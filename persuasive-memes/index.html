Persuasive Memes

Animated memes that argue for or against debate statements, each built from a tree of claims.

Each meme takes one side of a debate statement. It is built from a small tree of claims: the main claim becomes the meme's title, and every supporting claim becomes one frame, a short caption over a picture. Pictures were generated with Qwen-Image-2.1.
